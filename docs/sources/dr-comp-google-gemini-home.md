# Learn about Gemini for Home camera features

Gemini for Home camera features help you keep up with important events captured on your Nest Cameras and Doorbells. If you’re a Google Home app user who meets the eligibility requirements, you can use the following Gemini for Home camera features:

- **AI descriptions:**This feature provides more detailed short and long descriptions of recorded camera events.



  - For example, instead of the usual “activity or animal detected” label, the AI description may be ”dog barking and digging in garden.” [Learn more about recorded camera events](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/9233159).
  - AI descriptions can include certain types of sounds heard during an event, such as loud footsteps or dog barking, for cameras with the [microphone and audio recording turned on](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/9218407).

    - **Tip:** Audio is only captured and described when motion, people, animal, package, or vehicle first trigger a recorded camera event. Only non-verbal audio specified in the list is supported.
  - AI descriptions can be personalized with **Pet Memory** to recognize your pets by name.

    - For example, the description "Fido is playing in the living room" is used instead of "A dog is playing."
  - To enable this feature, go to **Ask Home** in the Google Home app and share your pet's name, animal type, and key details about them.

    - For example, “I have 2 pets. Loki is a brown golden doodle dog and Clarice is a gray siberian cat."

**Tips:**

  - **Pet Memory** only works on indoor camera models and works best for a single pet.
  - If you have multiple pets, the camera will attempt to distinguish between them as long as they look visually distinct (like having different coat colors). For best results, provide a clear description of those differences in **Ask Home**.
  - The device will provide its best effort to identify multiple pets and full accuracy isn’t guaranteed.

- **AI notifications:** This provides short versions of AI descriptions within camera and doorbell event notifications. This feature makes it easier to see what happened at a glance.
- **Home Brief:** This feature gives an overview of what happened at home each day.
- **Ask Home video history search:** This allows you to use natural language to search for a specific camera event.

### Supported sound descriptions

- Doorbell ringing
- Dog barking
- Footsteps
- Glass breaking
- Loud banging or crashing
- Loud siren sound or alarms
- Loud thudding or dropping sound
- Screaming or crying
- Gunshot

These detailed descriptions make it easier for you to search and review your recorded footage. You don’t need to tap every recorded event to find out what happened, just use specific keywords in Ask Home or check the Home Brief in the Activity tab. You’ll also receive short versions of these descriptions in AI notifications.

## Who can use Gemini for Home camera features

Gemini for Home camera features are rolling out to Google Home Premium subscribers with Advanced plans. You need to acknowledge the Gemini for Home disclosures before you can set up and use these features.

### Availability of Gemini for Home camera features

**Important:**

- Gemini for Home camera features are only available to adult Admins.
- These features are only available on:
  - Nest cameras set up in the Google Home app (launched in 2021 or later)
  - Legacy Nest cameras that have been transferred to the Google Home app. Learn more about [transferring devices to the Google Home app](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/13038234).

## How to check and use AI descriptions

Check all recorded camera events in the Activity ![Activity icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/I0uuMKIpStgN7bzP8FJNJSesVSrJONyxaIHQ) tab, search history results, or an individual camera’s video history view.

To go to your Activity tab and view the events:

1. Open the Google Home app ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/xR6BpxW016SfKZoAS3n9c35QWuVi6un0UEBe2BGEdqTjZZ9IK42OyMreSr_yRrlgRPI=h36).
2. Tap on a camera tile or at the bottom, tap Activity![Activity icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/I0uuMKIpStgN7bzP8FJNJSesVSrJONyxaIHQ).
3. Tap the event you’re interested in.
4. At the bottom left, tap Information ![information](https://storage.cloud.google.com/support-kms-prod/OAvQCpJIsh7xttUnODKbysK4PTJB1hbcH78k) to get a more detailed description of what happened.

For example, you might get “Person leaving package on porch” or “Car parking.”

**Tip:** To get audio events like “Baby crying” or “Siren,” [turn on your camera’s microphone and audio recording](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/9218407).

### How to search video history

To search for a specific recorded event, use the Ask Home search box available within your camera’s video history view or at the top of the Google Home app. To search events based on their descriptions, you can type phrases like:

- “Recent delivery.”
- “Unfamiliar visitor.”
- “Garage door opening.”
- “Did something eat my plants?”
- “When did the dog walker come by?”
- “Who let the dogs out?”

**Tips:**

- If you search your camera events for someone in your familiar face library, the results include any event in which their face was detected. These results will appear even if other people were there.
- If you turn on AI notifications, camera notifications should include richer notification text with short event descriptions. For example, you may receive a “Person seen” notification, which is later updated to a more descriptive description, “Person rides bike outside.”

### How to check your Home Brief

In addition to AI descriptions for each event, you will receive a Home Brief every evening in the Activity ![Activity icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/I0uuMKIpStgN7bzP8FJNJSesVSrJONyxaIHQ) tab that provides an overview of what happened at home that day. From the Home Brief, you can tap **See more** to get more details, or you can customize your Home Brief.

## How to provide feedback

To improve AI descriptions, use the Thumbs up ![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/12GnNzClefo1wuqz1hq8Ho1rEgwazUiCT0Fn) and Thumbs down ![thumbs down icon](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/4VIWWZsrDv2STOVFgDsHJWoPO_LN8j4D9yzRrBxMgTM7rF5_MUW5IYC7XZbrLEaFRg=h36) to provide feedback for every description.

1. Tap the camera event that you want to evaluate.
2. Tap Information ![information](https://storage.cloud.google.com/support-kms-prod/OAvQCpJIsh7xttUnODKbysK4PTJB1hbcH78k).
3. A standard feedback question will be available under the detailed description. Tap either Thumbs up ![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/12GnNzClefo1wuqz1hq8Ho1rEgwazUiCT0Fn) or Thumbs down ![thumbs down icon](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/4VIWWZsrDv2STOVFgDsHJWoPO_LN8j4D9yzRrBxMgTM7rF5_MUW5IYC7XZbrLEaFRg=h36).
4. Follow the on-screen instructions.
5. In “Event Feedback,” answer all applicable questions. You can write in the text box to help explain your feedback.
