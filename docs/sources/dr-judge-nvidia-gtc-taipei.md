If you’re building vision AI applications, NVIDIA GTC Taipei has a lot in store. Factories, autonomous agents, video analytics, synthetic data generation, and AI models — here’s what’s new.

- **NVIDIA Factory Operations Blueprint (FOX) Gives Factories a New AI Brain** — a reference design for building centralized factory manager agents that orchestrate fleets of industrial AI agents at scale. [Learn more.](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/factory-operations-fox-blueprint-ai-brain)

- **NVIDIA Metropolis Blueprint for Video Search and Summarization (VSS) 3** now ships with a set of new agent skills — letting you build and customize video analytics AI agents faster with simple natural language prompts. General availability drops June 8th. [Read the blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/transform-video-into-instantly-searchable-actionable-intelligence-with-ai-agents-and-skills/). [Explore VSS skills](https://github.com/NVIDIA-AI-Blueprints/video-search-and-summarization/tree/main/skills).

- **NVIDIA DeepStream now supports JetPack 7.2 and new agent skills** that let you generate complete video ingestion pipelines from natural language prompts. [Read the blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-build-vision-ai-pipelines-using-deepstream-coding-agents/). [Explore DeepStream skills](https://github.com/NVIDIA-AI-IOT/DeepStream_Coding_Agent/tree/main/skills/deepstream-dev).

- **NVIDIA Cosmos 3 launches as the world’s first fully open omni-model for physical AI** — combining vision reasoning, world generation, and action prediction in one foundation model. [Read the blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/develop-physical-ai-reasoning-world-and-action-models-with-nvidia-cosmos-3). [Download Cosmos 3](https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/cosmos3)

- **NVIDIA Releases a Major Collection of Open Source Physical AI Skills** [Read the announcement.](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-releases-major-collection-of-open-source-agent-tools-and-skills-for-physical-ai) Explore the [defect image generation skill](https://github.com/NVIDIA/skills/tree/main/skills/physical-ai-defect-image-generation) and [video augmentation skill](https://github.com/NVIDIA/skills/tree/main/skills/physical-ai-video-data-augmentation).
