# Three-minute live demo

Open https://team-41-vss.thecosmoslabs.com/app/ with the latest asset version.

1. Show the real highway footage and the coverage banner. Explain that this deployment has one scene and three cameras, with 180 indexed segments over 300 seconds.
2. Say or type “There are cars on the road.” Show the TRUE verdict and the detection persistence evidence. Open the trace receipt and Weave link.
3. Type “Toronto traffic is moving freely.” Show the explicit place claim and the FALSE verdict from Nashville metadata. Explain that traffic conditions are evaluated separately, and that insufficient cameras remain unproven.
4. Try a towing or braking claim. Show the model calls and evidence; do not promise a positive verdict. Explain that grounding, crop detection, visibility, and quorum determine whether it can be settled.
5. Enable stock VSS comparison and show the original answer next to the evidence-based verdict.
6. Open Witness stand and Bench. Show measured results and exclusions, emphasizing that the small smoke evaluation is not a general accuracy estimate.

All services and footage are real. Cached detection sidecars and condition probes are actual prior inference results, not fake answers. The app identifies cached evidence and timestamps it.

Before presenting, verify voice transcription, exhibit image and video playback, stock comparison, and the latest coverage status. Full multi-scene coverage remains dependent on the organizers restoring missing footage.
