# Real-Time Video Agents Hack

Build agents that unlock the value of video. Explore video understanding, semantic search, and real-time detection, then show a working prototype at the end of the day.

[Submit your project](https://tokensand.com/vastsf/submit) [Browse projects](https://tokensand.com/vastsf/projects) [Join Discord](https://bit.ly/discord-17)

Opens 10:00 AMPT · Deadline 4:30 PMPT

One project per team · Up to 4 people

[![Real-Time Video Agents Hack](https://images.lumacdn.com/uploads/zz/83f5b88b-730e-4089-a567-251727cd4da0.png)](https://luma.com/vastsf)

## What you’re building

Build a video agent that searches footage, answers questions, spots events, or takes action. One idea, working by the end of the day.

Pick a specific use case: “flag someone missing a hard hat,” rather than “watch for safety issues.” Adapt your idea to the footage available on build day.

Search depends on what the model’s prompt captures in each video description. You can ingest video with a different prompt on build day.

[Video search overview](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qUrT0QaBGS6mXxHTEG2vnLM2pT1KY4RD/view?usp=sharing) [YOLO object detection](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo11#overview) [CoreWeave GPUs](https://coreweave.com/) [ARIA research agent](https://wandb.ai/site/agent/)

## What to submit

- A public GitHub repository
- A short demo video with a shareable link
- What you built and the tools you used
- Your team’s names and contact emails

A working website and project screenshot are welcome. Build your project during the event.
