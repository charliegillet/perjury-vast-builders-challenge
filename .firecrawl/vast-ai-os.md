## How the AI OS Works

Every capability in the VAST AI OS runs on a single architecture: DASE (Disaggregated Shared-Everything). DASE separates compute from capacity so each can scale independently, and it gives every node access to the full global dataset. That's how the AI OS keeps adding capabilities — database, event streaming, vector search, agent orchestration — without compromise.

DataStore

## Unified Storage

Every workflow starts here. Enterprise data, whether block, file, or object, is written to the DataStore, a single exabyte-scale foundation that consolidates all of it without the tiering and silos that slow AI.

`Best data reduction. Get the most value out of every PB of capacity you deploy.`

## Common Questions About the AI OS

What does the VAST AI OS replace in my current infrastructure?

Copy link to clipboard

The AI OS is one unified platform that replaces dozens of point solutions, dramatically reducing complexity and vendor management overhead. Think about simplifying:

- Multiple storage tiers and vendors

- Separate vector databases

- External streaming platforms

- Complex ETL pipelines and data preparation tools

- Disparate MLOps and agent deployment systems


Who is this for: individuals, enterprises, or specific industries?

Copy link to clipboard

**AI Builders & Cloud Providers:** Organizations creating and deploying large-scale AI models and cloud platforms need extreme performance, scalability, and advanced tooling to manage their next-gen AI services and agentic systems.

**Enterprise Leadership:** C-suite executives leveraging AI for their competitive advantage and business transformations need strategic solutions that deliver measurable ROI, robust security, and simplified infrastructure management.

**Technical Implementers:** AI engineers and architects building and maintaining AI/ML infrastructure need scalable platforms with efficient MLOps tools, simplified integration, and support for advanced AI capabilities, like real-time processing and vector databases for RAG.

What are the costs?

Copy link to clipboard

Learn more about our [flexible deployment and consumption options](https://www.vastdata.com/platform/gemini).

Where can I deploy the VAST AI OS?

Copy link to clipboard

**Runs everywhere AI lives**: on-premises data centers, public clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP, Oracle), edge locations, or hybrid environments. Software-first architecture adapts to your infrastructure, not the other way around.

**Hardware Compatibility**: Works efficiently on commodity hardware platforms with established partnerships including HPE, Cisco, Dell, Lenovo, [and more](https://www.vastdata.com/partners) to ensure smooth deployment across different environments.

How does it integrate with my existing infrastructure?

Copy link to clipboard

**Protocol Support:** Natively supports all major enterprise protocols including NFS, SMB, S3, NVMe over TCP, SQL, and Kafka-compatible streaming, so your existing applications and workflows continue to work.

**System Integration:** Integrates with existing Kubernetes clusters and bridges legacy applications through real-time message brokers. Built on industry standards like Model Context Protocol (MCP) for maximum interoperability with current AI tools.

**Security Continuity:** Unified identity management and end-to-end encryption work with your existing security frameworks while extending governance across the entire AI environment.
