# The VAST Builders Challenge

​The VAST Builders Challenge is a three-city technical event series bringing together engineers and builders across New York, London, and San Francisco to build production-ready AI systems. Hosted in collaboration with CoreWeave, SpaceXAI, NVIDIA, and Weights & Biases.

​Applications are required. Space is limited. Registration closes one week prior to the event.
