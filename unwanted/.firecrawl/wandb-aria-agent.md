# CoreWeave  ARIA

Whether you're building an agent or training a model, ARIA (AI Research and Iteration Agent) walks you through the AI Loop. It analyzes your experiments, proposes what to try next, and launches it with your approval, showing its evidence with in-chat visualizations at every step.

[Get started](https://:%20id.coreweave.com/login) [Watch the demo](https://www.coreweave.com/products/coreweave-forge/aria#)

### Drive continuous improvement

Analyze results, propose the next experiment, launch it, repeat. Set it up once as an Automation and the loop runs on every finished experiment. Connect your codebase and ARIA drafts each change as a PR, so nothing ships without a human reviewing it.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62ba1fb86485b6d5029975c4/6ab11b6e6b815b52e631e290_Pace.svg)

### Accelerate deeper analysis

Training models and building agents is data-heavy work, and understanding it means visualizing your data, not just viewing numbers. ARIA lives in the Forge dashboard and answers with live visualizations rendered in the chat, so you follow its reasoning clearly. When a finding matters, it creates persistent reports and dashboard views your team can keep using.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62ba1fb86485b6d5029975c4/6ab474965dd6c9eba3237d2d_Community.svg)

### Shared team knowledge

Forge is organized around projects, and so is ARIA's memory. As your team works, ARIA remembers what the experiments showed and what you decided, scoped to the project rather than to one person's session. Every conversation starts from shared team learnings.

Runs the AI Loop

Describe what you want to learn and ARIA forms hypotheses, launches experiments, evaluates results, and recommends the next iteration. For agent builders that means prompt and model changes tested against real traffic. For model trainers it means training jobs launched on your infrastructure, from sweeps to fine-tuning and RL. You approve each launch and review each change on its own branch, while ARIA handles the work in between.
