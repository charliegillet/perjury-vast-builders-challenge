_New platform capabilities announced at VerkadaOne extend real-time visibility, automated deterrence, and customizable AI workflows across the enterprise._

SAN MATEO, Calif., Sept. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- [Verkada](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4776895-1&h=367369331&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verkada.com%2F&a=Verkada), a leader in AI-powered physical security and operations, today unveiled new capabilities for vehicle fleets, enterprise sound systems, and building operations during its annual customer conference, [VerkadaOne](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4776895-1&h=2552933084&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verkada.com%2Fverkada-one%2F&a=VerkadaOne).

**Protecting people and assets in motion with Verkada Transportation**

Verkada is extending enterprise protection to people and assets in transit with its new [Verkada Transportation](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4776895-1&h=188584658&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verkada.com%2Fblog%2Fintroducing-verkada-transportation%2F&a=Verkada+Transportation) product line. With three ruggedized camera models, a 5G and Wi-Fi vehicle gateway, integration with vehicle diagnostics, and an enhanced Command experience, Verkada's platform will ensure every vehicle is safe, secure and ready for transit – and passenger privacy with face blurring. Security teams can access footage from moving or parked vehicles, review fleet-wide driving events to track overall safety and performance, and search by GPS location, driving behavior, or vehicle health.

**Streamlining cloud-managed audio with Verkada Sound Systems**

Verkada launched [Sound Systems](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4776895-1&h=1483050480&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verkada.com%2Fblog%2Fintroducing-verkada-sound-systems%2F&a=Sound+Systems), bringing cloud-based control to enterprise audio. Sound Systems offers a single interface for enterprises to manage every part of an organization's audio environment – from hardware to ambient music, and even announcements – while eliminating the need for separate apps, local consoles, and vendor portals that enterprise sound systems have traditionally required. The lineup includes Network Audio Players to connect existing third-party speakers and amplifiers to Command, as well as Network Speakers with plug-and-play Power over Ethernet (PoE) connectivity for simplified deployment.

**Introducing Catalyst, Verkada's MCP for Physical AI**

Verkada introduced [Catalyst](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4776895-1&h=3694539050&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verkada.com%2Fblog%2Fopen-platform-updates-september-2026%2F&a=Catalyst), a read-only Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration for its Command platform that enables customers to connect approved third-party AI platforms like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. With Catalyst, teams can create custom operational workflows, reports, and integrations in minutes using natural language – from confirming shelves are stocked to triggering an IT ticket when a camera or sensor loses its network connection. Catalyst will roll out as a read-only tool, ensuring organizations retain control over their agents and workflows.
